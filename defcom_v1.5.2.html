Defcom v1.5.2Pre-DHB + Post-DHB decision support

Bug disposition, from triage to DHB.

Use Pre-DHB to determine whether a defect can be safely handled within the current release or should go to DHB. Post-DHB reuses the Pre-DHB record, confirms DHB approved deferral, then evaluates Issue Impact × Change Risk.
